# Public authority requests for personal data

This procedure applies to requests from public authorities for personal data
held by Co za dzban, including data received through Meta. It supports the
four public authority request controls in [Meta's Data Handling Questions](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/resp-plat-initiatives/individual-processes/data-handling-questions/questions-preview/).
The app administrator is responsible for following it. Do not put requests,
personal data, or case records in this Git repository.
Use the [restricted case record template](PUBLIC_AUTHORITY_REQUEST_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md)
for each request, including requests that result in no disclosure.

## Intake and legality review

1. Record the date received, the requesting authority, its contact details,
   the data sought, the stated purpose, and any deadline in a restricted case
   record. Assign a case identifier.
2. Verify the sender and the authenticity of the request using an independent
   contact channel. Do not use contact details in an unverified request alone.
3. Before disclosing data, review the authority's legal power, jurisdiction,
   required form or order, scope, and any applicable notice or confidentiality
   requirements. Seek qualified legal advice when the basis or scope is unclear.
4. Record the conclusion and its legal reasoning. If the request has no valid
   basis, do not disclose data and record the response.

## Challenge and minimum disclosure

5. If a request appears unlawful or overly broad, seek clarification, narrowing,
   withdrawal, or review through an available legal process. Consult qualified
   legal counsel as needed. Record the challenge, participants, and outcome.
6. For a valid request, identify only the records and fields needed to satisfy
   it. Exclude unrelated users, dates, fields, and secrets such as access tokens
   unless their disclosure is specifically required by law. Use a secure delivery
   channel and keep a copy or description of exactly what was disclosed.

## Documentation and access

7. In the restricted case record, preserve the original request, authenticity
   checks, legal analysis, people involved and their roles, challenges, decisions,
   response, disclosure inventory, dates, and delivery method. Record a decision
   even when no data was disclosed.
8. Limit access to the case record to those handling the request. Retain it only
   for the period required by applicable law or needed to establish the response,
   then dispose of it securely. Review any later request to modify or delete the
   related personal data against applicable legal obligations.

These steps are operating instructions, not a record that a particular request
was received or handled. Answer Meta's questionnaire according to the process
actually in use at the time of submission.
